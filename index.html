about

I'm Hemlata Saini — a Senior Product Designer and Associate Art Director based in Delhi, India. Over a decade, I've designed scalable enterprise platforms, extended design systems, and simplified complex digital workflows for global organizations.

My work lives at the intersection of systems thinking, UX strategy, and pixel-perfect execution. I've designed products used by hundreds of internal users at companies like Marriott International and Omantel, tackled data-heavy B2B dashboards for Shell and ADNOC, and built brand-new digital ecosystems for startups and luxury brands across three continents.

Whether I'm re-architecting a fragmented 2,000-page telecoms platform or designing a mobile-first recruitment ecosystem for the American transport industry — the throughline is always the same: create structured clarity from complex chaos.

→
Systems Before Screens
Individual pages don't scale. Reusable, well-structured design systems do. I always look for the underlying pattern before touching a single component.
→
Complexity Is the Brief, Not the Barrier
Enterprise platforms are inherently messy. I thrive where workflows are fragmented, stakeholder needs conflict, and legacy systems demand creative constraint.
→
Clarity Is a Design Outcome
Whether it's a 500-screen reservation system or a luxury e-commerce page — if the user has to think too hard, the design isn't finished yet.
→
Cross-functional Delivery Is Part of the Job
Design that can't survive handoff isn't production-ready. I work across product, engineering, QA, and business as a matter of course.
